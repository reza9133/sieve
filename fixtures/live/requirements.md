# Community Pool Roof Renovation - Mandatory Requirements

This document defines the three mandatory requirements for the tender. A proposal is
compliant only if its own text satisfies every one of them. Price, design quality and
the bidder's reputation are judged elsewhere and must not be considered here.

R1 (Delivery): The bidder must commit to completing the entire roof renovation within
90 calendar days of the award date.

R2 (Insurance): The bidder must state that it holds liability insurance with coverage of
at least 1,000,000 USD for the duration of the work.

R3 (Warranty): The bidder must offer a written warranty of at least 5 years covering all
materials and workmanship.
